Frequently Asked Admissions Questions

Can we attend a trial lecture before taking admission?

+
Absolutely! We encourage parents and students to attend our 2-day free trial class. This gives the student complete clarity on the teaching pace, atmosphere, and faculty approach before taking admission.

What documents are required during admission?

+
Please bring 2 passport-size photographs of the student and a photocopy of the latest school/college progress card (mark sheet) to verify eligibility for merit scholarship concessions.

Is parking available at the Bytco Point campus?

+
Yes, Ishwar Sankul has dedicated two-wheeler and bicycle parking space inside the complex for students attending morning and evening batches.
