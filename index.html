Common Queries

Frequently Asked Questions by Parents

Have questions before joining? Here are straightforward answers to the most common queries we receive.

What is the average batch size at Perfect Coaching Classes?

+
We strictly maintain small batches capped between 20 to 25 students. This ensures that every child receives individualized attention, comfortable seating, and personalized doubt resolution without feeling lost in overcrowded lecture halls.

How do parents stay informed about student attendance and test marks?

+
Attendance is recorded daily. Furthermore, marks from every Sunday test are communicated directly to parents via SMS and WhatsApp report cards within 48 hours of paper evaluation. We also conduct scheduled Parent-Teacher Meetings (PTMs) every term.

What happens if a student misses a lecture due to school events or illness?

+
We offer dedicated backup and doubt-clearing sessions on Saturdays and Sunday afternoons. Subject teachers personally walk the student through missed concepts and provide corresponding classroom notes and worksheets.

Can students enroll solely for the Sunday Test Series program?

+
Yes! Our famous Sunday Test Series is open to external candidates from Class 10th SSC, ICSE, 12th HSC, and MHT-CET. It includes 35+ weekly mock exams under strict board examination conditions, complete with detailed teacher feedback.

Are printed study materials and formula books included in the fees?

+
Yes, all enrolled classroom students receive complete chapter-wise theory booklets, 10-year past paper question banks, and formula pocket guides at no additional charge.

Are installment fee payment options available?

+
Yes, we provide a 3-part flexible installment plan spread across the academic term. We also offer merit-based fee concessions of up to 25%–30% based on the student's previous school examination scores.
